KairryClips Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KairryClips ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how KairryClips collects, uses, and shares information in connection with our content creation activities and our use of third-party social media platforms.

KairryClips is an independent content creator brand publishing short-form video clips on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. We curate highlight clips from livestream broadcasts of partnered content creators, published with their explicit permission.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to this Privacy Policy page
  • Users who contact us via email
  • Content creators whose material we feature
  • Viewers who interact with our content on third-party platforms

2. Information We Collect

KairryClips operates a minimal data collection model. We do not maintain a commercial website with user accounts, registration, or tracking systems.

2.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact us via email (e.g., business inquiries, copyright questions, partnership requests), we receive:

  • Your email address
  • Your name (if included in your communication)
  • The contents of your message
  • Any attachments you choose to share

2.2 Information from Third-Party Platforms

When you interact with our content on YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram, those platforms collect data about you according to their own privacy policies. We do not have direct access to this data beyond aggregate analytics provided by each platform.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use cookies on this Privacy Policy page
  • We do not run analytics or tracking scripts on our hosted documents
  • We do not collect IP addresses, device information, or location data
  • We do not collect payment information
  • We do not maintain user accounts or login systems on our own infrastructure

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries: We use email correspondence to communicate with business partners, content creators, and viewers who reach out to us.
  • To verify content permissions: We retain communication records related to content licensing and creator permissions to demonstrate compliance with our partnership agreements.
  • To comply with legal obligations: We may retain records as required by Indonesian law or by the policies of our partner platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Meta).

We do not use personal information for:

  • Marketing or advertising to you
  • Selling or sharing with third parties for commercial purposes
  • Automated decision-making or profiling
  • Building user profiles for targeted content

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties, except in these limited circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, valid court order, or valid government request.
  • Platform compliance: When required to comply with the official policies of YouTube, TikTok, or Meta.

5. Third-Party Services

KairryClips publishes content via the following platforms, each governed by their own privacy practices:

  • YouTube (operated by Google LLC) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • TikTok (operated by TikTok Inc./ByteDance) — tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Instagram (operated by Meta Platforms) — help.instagram.com/519522125107875

We use the official APIs of these platforms (YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram Graph API) to publish content. Authentication occurs through OAuth, and we do not access or store viewer personal data through these integrations.

6. Content Creator Information

For creators whose content we feature:

  • We retain documentation of explicit permission to use their content
  • We display credit (creator name, links to original sources) on every published clip
  • We provide an unconditional opt-out mechanism: any featured creator may request removal of their content at any time by emailing kairryyclip@gmail.com
  • We comply with verified takedown requests within 48 hours of receipt

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary:

  • Email correspondence: Up to 2 years from last interaction, then deleted.
  • Content licensing records: Retained for the duration of content publication, plus 1 year afterwards.
  • Anonymous platform analytics: Retained according to each third-party platform's policy.

8. Children's Privacy

KairryClips content is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact kairryyclip@gmail.com and we will delete the information promptly.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable security practices to protect the limited information we hold:

  • Email correspondence is protected by Gmail's encryption and security infrastructure
  • We do not store sensitive personal or financial data
  • API credentials are stored securely and not exposed publicly

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but strive to use commercially reasonable means to protect your information.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information.
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your information.

To exercise these rights, email kairryyclip@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days of receiving a verified request.

11. International Data Transfers

KairryClips operates from Indonesia. The third-party platforms we use (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) operate globally and may transfer data internationally. By interacting with our content on these platforms, you acknowledge that data may be processed in countries outside your own.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective Date" at the top reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be communicated via update notes on our YouTube channel description or this hosted document.
